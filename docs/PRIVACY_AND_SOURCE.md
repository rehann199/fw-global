# Privacy and Source

## What this repository contains

- A written case study of the FW Global website: product overview, architecture, engineering notes.
- Screenshots of the public pages, captured from the live site.
- Diagrams written in Mermaid.

## What it does not contain

- Production source code. It is maintained privately and is not copied here.
- Sensitive configuration exists in the real project (admin password, storage credentials, email-service settings) and is deliberately excluded. No values appear in this repository.
- Screenshots or descriptions of the private admin area beyond a high-level summary.
- Personal data. Screenshots show only public marketing and catalogue content.

## Public information

Product names, store badges and marketing copy in the screenshots are publicly visible at https://fwglobal.co. Third-party store badges and brand names belong to their owners.
